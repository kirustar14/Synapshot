# Prompt for Paper / Paper AI

Use the supplied assets from `Synapshot_Paper_Landing_Kit_v6` to create the final Synapshot landing page design.

## Primary goal
Design a premium, minimal, warm editorial landing page for a project called **Synapshot**, intended for a hackathon design track. The page should feel handcrafted, calm, and distinctive — not like a generic AI-generated SaaS landing page.

## Visual direction
Use `assets/room_base_full_scroll.png` as the primary scene direction.
Use `references/hero_framing_reference.png` and `references/full_scroll_layout_reference.png` as framing / layout references.

The final landing page should feel like **one continuous sunlit room** from top to bottom.

### Keep these compositional priorities
- a visible tall window on the left
- sheer curtain presence on the left
- large open text area on the left / left-center
- the human shadow silhouette on the upper-right wall
- the neural brain inside the silhouette head area
- the table, books, bowl, and plant grounding the right side
- a calmer lower continuation of the room for the rest of the landing page content

### Avoid
- split-screen startup-page feeling
- a giant white gradient panel swallowing the room
- over-styled neon effects
- extra decorative widgets
- generic stock-medical aesthetic
- cluttered layout or too many blocks

## Landing page content structure
Keep the page simple and elegant:
- top left wordmark: `synapshot`
- top right one button: `Login`
- hero headline: `Ongoing insight for recovering minds.`
- supporting copy: `AI-powered neurological check-ins between visits, using simple cognitive tests analyzed over video.`
- lower section heading: `How Synapshot works`
- three steps: `Check in`, `Understand change`, `Stay informed`
- closing statement: `Recovery doesn’t stop between appointments. Neither should our understanding of it.`
- footer disclaimer indicating research prototype status

Keep all text as actual interface content, not image decoration.

## Motion direction (for design intent / spec)
The page should be designed with motion in mind:
- curtains gently billow
- the plant subtly sways
- a few leaves drift occasionally
- sunlight and shadow shift softly and physically coherently
- the brain glows / traces elegantly

These should feel coordinated by the same environment, like a soft breeze moving through the room.

## Design character
The design should score well on:
- hierarchy
- legibility
- consistency
- contrast
- ease of use
- personal craft and character

It should feel premium and intentional, not overly “AI slop” or kiddish.

## Output objective
Produce a clean landing-page design spec / mockup that can then be translated into code, preserving the room framing and leaving enough guidance for motion implementation.
